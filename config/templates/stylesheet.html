Kýrauga brand guide

Calm tools for seeing place.

Kýrauga is a personal photography, multimedia, and mapping project shaped by Icelandic landscapes, memory, weather, and quiet attention.

Typography

AXIS-ExtraBold

Use the display face for the Kýrauga name, major headings, and short identity statements. Let body copy stay calm and readable in Inter.

Body sample

A route bends across damp moss and pale stone. The image is less about arrival than the small evidence gathered along the way.

Tone

  • Calm: leave room around important things.
  • Exploratory: invite movement, maps, routes, and discovery.
  • Nostalgic: let materials feel remembered, not polished flat.
  • Vulnerable: write plainly and keep the human trace visible.

Photography

Let photographs breathe.

Images should feel observed rather than packaged. Prefer generous scale, quiet captions, natural aspect ratios, and small brand gestures that do not overpower the photograph.

Do

Use `object-cover`, stable aspect ratios, and captions that add place, route, memory, or context.

Avoid

Heavy filters, crowded overlays, decorative frames, or brand color washes over every image.

Brand cue

Use the green as a small marker, badge, underline, or focus detail beside the photograph.
